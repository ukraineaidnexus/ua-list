Its only purpose is cross-listing, e.g. an NGO registered both in Ukraine and abroad: type: ua-ngo also: [foreign-ngo] ...which puts it on /ngos/ and /foreign-ngos/ both, while its detail page stays at the single canonical URL its `type` implies.
